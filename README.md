### Projects

**[IMC-Prosperity-4-RiftWalkers](https://github.com/aryaarun0910-debug/IMC-Prosperity-4-RiftWalkers)**
Market-making and mean-reversion trading engine written in dependency-free Python, with a custom
walk-forward backtester and position-limit risk controls. Ranked 6th in the world on the Round 1
manual trading round, and top 3.7% globally against 18,803 teams.

**[ewb_bus_routing_project](https://github.com/aryaarun0910-debug/ewb_bus_routing_project)**
Predictive bus routing for Ladywood, Birmingham. An XGBoost demand model and a capacitated
vehicle-routing optimiser behind a FastAPI and React dashboard, validated against 2.3M real London
bus boardings, plus an FPGA subsystem in SystemVerilog. Reached the Grand Final of the EWB
Engineering for People Design Challenge.

**[Vesuvius-Challenge](https://github.com/aryaarun0910-debug/Vesuvius-Challenge)**
A 3D CNN ensemble in PyTorch for surface segmentation on scroll-volume CT data. The interesting part
is the validation: topology-aware losses that catch the merge and split errors Dice-based training
scores right through. Open, self-directed research rather than a competition entry.
